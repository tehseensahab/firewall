Write for Us

FirewallSync publishes practical, field-tested security writing for engineering teams — not vendor whitepapers, not listicles. If you’ve shipped a fix, run an incident, or built a process that actually held up under pressure, we want to hear about it.

What we publish

  • Postmortems and lessons learned (anonymized details are fine)
  • Concrete how-tos: rollout plans, tooling comparisons, architecture decisions
  • Opinionated takes on practices the industry treats as settled but shouldn’t

What we don’t publish

  • Generic “top 10 security tips” content
  • Thinly-veiled product pitches
  • Anything you can’t back with specifics

Byline & links

Accepted posts get a full author byline and one contextual link back to your site or profile. We don’t do paid placements or sponsored posts dressed up as articles — see our advertising page if that’s what you’re looking for instead.

How to pitch

Send a two- or three-sentence pitch and a draft or outline to editors@firewallsync.com. Include your name, a short bio, and any links to previous writing. We reply to every pitch within a week.

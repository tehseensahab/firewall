## Pitches and guest posts

Send a short pitch and a draft or outline to **editors@firewallsync.com**. See [Write for Us](/write-for-us/) for what we publish.

## Corrections and tips

Found an error, or have a topic we should cover? Email **editors@firewallsync.com** with the article URL and what should change. See our [corrections policy](/corrections/).

## Advertising and placements

Email **contact@tehseenarbab.com** with your site, the proposed topic and a writing sample. See [Advertise](/advertise/).

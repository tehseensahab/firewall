FirewallSync is a static website. We keep data collection to a minimum.

## What we collect

- **No accounts, no comments, no forms.** You can read everything without giving us any personal information.
- **No advertising or tracking cookies.** We do not set cookies.
- **Theme preference.** If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## Hosting

The site is served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP addresses and request details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and to handle your request, such as a pitch or a correction. We do not add you to a mailing list.

## Links to other sites

Articles link to vendor documentation and other sources. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

FirewallSync accepts a limited number of guest contributions from writers and agencies in the security and IT space.

**What we're looking for:** original, specific writing on cybersecurity, IT operations, or engineering process — no thin AI-generated filler, no more than one contextual link back to your site.

**What we don't accept:** gambling, adult, or unrelated affiliate content; guest posts with more than one promotional link; anything that reads like a press release.

To inquire about a placement, email **contact@tehseenarbab.com** with your site, the proposed topic, and a writing sample.
